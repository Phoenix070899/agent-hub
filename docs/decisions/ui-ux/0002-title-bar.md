# A 52 pt title bar holds the mark, a centred search field, and the account usage

Decided: 2026-10-18
Status: accepted

The window hides the system title bar; a 52 pt bar replaces it.
The traffic lights sit vertically centred in that bar (`TrafficLights.swift`), and the bar's leading content starts at 86 pt, or 16 pt in full screen.
On the left: the Echo mark and the name "Echo".
In the centre: a 520 by 30 pt search field, white in light mode, that opens Quick Open; it shows ⌘K.
On the right: "N Workers running" and the plan usage, or "Echo daemon running" when there are no sessions yet.
