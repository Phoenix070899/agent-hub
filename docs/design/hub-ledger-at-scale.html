How it holds up as numbers grow

These are the rules the mock follows. They decide what folds, what stays open, and what moves into the session tabs.

Needs you never folds

Each item gets one line of who and what, then the command and its buttons on one row. Long commands truncate and show in full on hover. It stays readable up to about six; past that, ⌘↩ and ⌘⌫ act on the top item.

Running is one line per Worker

Id, profile, Task, quiet time. Only the selected Worker shows its progress line. Sorted by quiet time, longest first, so a stuck Worker rises to the top on its own.

Done keeps the last few

Failed and blocked sort first in red, then the three newest. Everything else is behind Show all, which is where report history from the backlog can live later.

Other sessions surface in two places

A marker count on their tab, and an Also waiting line under your Needs you. You never have to visit a session to learn it needs you.

Session tabs sort themselves

Sessions that need you come first, then live ones by Worker count. Stopped sessions fold into one menu at the end, so the bar holds about seven live repos on a laptop screen.

The Orchestrator is the top row

With tabs gone, it’s pinned above the ledger and always one click away. Clicking any Worker row swaps the terminal; clicking the Orchestrator row brings it back.
